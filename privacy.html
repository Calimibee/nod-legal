Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. If this English version and the Turkish version differ, the Turkish version prevails for users in Türkiye.

In short: Nod helps people in a household check in with each other (“bi’ sor”) before a big purchase. We never ask for your bank account, card or location. We show no ads, we don’t track you across apps, and we don’t sell your data. We only process what the app needs to work.

1. Controller

The controller for the Nod app is Nazif Anıl Erduran (“Nod”, “we”), İstanbul, Türkiye. This policy provides the information required by Art. 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and Art. 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”). Contact: calimibee1@gmail.com

3. Legal bases

  • Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): account, households, asks, decisions, messages, photos and Plus features.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): security, abuse prevention, usage limits, debugging.
  • Legal obligation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c), KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)): statutory retention and lawful requests.
  • Consent: notifications are only sent if you allow them in iOS; you can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

4. What your household can see

People in your household see your name and role. Asks you send or receive are shared with the people involved. Depending on the “Who sees history” setting, either partners only or the whole household sees past asks. Joining another household does not expose data from your other households.

6. Retention

  • Account and household data are kept while your account exists.
  • When Nod Plus ends nothing is deleted; some features are simply switched off.
  • You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account). Deletion is immediate and removes: your name, email address, your sign-in record and all sessions (including the Sign in with Apple link and pending email codes), push tokens and notification preferences, notification history and usage counters. Your household memberships are removed.
  • Asks, decisions, messages and photos you added to asks stay in your household’s history under “Deleted user”, no longer linked to your name or email, because they are part of other members’ history. Email us if you want these deleted too.
  • Deleted data also drops out of the database restore history automatically within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Purchase records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat under their legal obligations.
  • Technical logs are kept only briefly, for security and debugging.
  • Records subject to statutory retention are kept for that period.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR Art. 15–22 and KVKK Art. 11 you have the right to access your data and receive a copy (including in a portable format), to rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, to know which third parties received your data, to contest a decision based solely on automated processing, and to compensation.

Email calimibee1@gmail.com from the address you use in the app. We reply free of charge within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Board, KVKK).

8. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Photos are stored in a private bucket and shown only through short-lived signed URLs. Server keys are never embedded in the app. Invite codes are single-use and expire after 48 hours.

9. Children

Nod is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and announce significant changes in the app.
